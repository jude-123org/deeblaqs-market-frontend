Quick answers to the most common questions about DeeBlaq’s Market.

How do I place an order?

Create a free account and log in. Add money to your wallet, put the product you want in your cart, then tap Pay from wallet.

How do I fund my wallet?

Open Wallet, send your payment to the bank account shown there, then enter the amount and your payment reference and tap Submit deposit. Once we confirm it, your balance updates.

How do I receive my order?

After you pay, your order appears under My orders in your Wallet. We send your delivery details to you and mark the order as delivered.

Can I get help before ordering?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp at 09133237159 or call 09133237159 or 07082310604. We are available 24/7.

What if I have a problem with my order?

If an account fails to log in within the first 24 hours, we offer a one-time replacement. Tell us on WhatsApp as soon as it happens.

Can I get a refund?

Because these are digital assets, refunds are not provided once login details have been sent. The 24-hour replacement policy still applies.

Where can I see my orders?

Log in and open Wallet. Your deposits, balance and orders are all listed there.

Can I order something that is not listed?

Yes. Use Custom Orders and tell us what you need. We source it for you.
